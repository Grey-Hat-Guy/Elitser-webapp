From Foundations to Legacy

Our Journey Across 20 Years

2006

The Spark

Elitser partnered with ManageEngine in 2006 to break free from rigid vendor practices, offering genuine value and robust IT helpdesk solutions to the UAE market.

An early milestone win with a prominent Abu Dhabi bank ignited two decades of exponential growth.

2006 - 2026

Building a Legacy of Trust

What began as a local vendor partnership evolved into an extended enterprise family. Together, Elitser and ManageEngine weathered economic shifts and market disruptions with transparency and mutual respect.

The Next Horizon

Looking Ahead

Driving AI, cloud adoption, cybersecurity, and Enterprise Service Management (ESM). Scaling forward with targets to onboard 100+ new enterprise clients annually across the region.
