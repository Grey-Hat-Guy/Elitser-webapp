The Elitser & ManageEngine Story

The Spark (2006)

Back in 2006, when the UAE's technological landscape was rapidly evolving, Elitser set out to find a visionary partner. They found it in ManageEngine. In a market dominated by rigid and costly IT vendors, the partnership launched with a different philosophy: deliver genuine value, fair pricing, and robust features without compromise.

The turning point came with an early, high-stakes win, saving a frustrated Abu Dhabi bank from restrictive vendor practices with ManageEngine’s helpdesk solution. That single success sparked an extraordinary journey.

Building a Legacy of Trust

Over the past 20 years, what started as a simple vendor-partner agreement evolved into an extended family. Avoiding a transactional mindset, Elitser and ManageEngine weathered economic shifts, market disruptions, and a global pandemic together, bound by mutual respect, transparency, and a shared vision.

Looking Ahead: The Next Horizon

As artificial intelligence, cloud adoption, cybersecurity, and Enterprise Service Management (ESM) reshape the future, Elitser and ManageEngine are gearing up for their next chapter. With regional goals to onboard 100+ new customers annually and expand their global footprint, the mission remains unchanged:

"Products may start the relationship, but shared values make it last for decades. Here is to celebrating 20 years of partnership, the people who made it possible, and an even brighter future ahead."

Today

The partnership proudly serves over 850 enterprise customers across 20+ industries [spanning 4 to 5 of the top 10 enterprises in most sectors] with a 94%+ retention rate and over 3,000 successful projects delivered.

Unmatched Expertise

Powered by 100+ specialized consultants out of Elitser's 350+ workforce, positioning ManageEngine behind critical compliance frameworks like ISO 20000, ISO 27001, and COBIT.
